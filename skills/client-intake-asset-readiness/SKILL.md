# Client Intake and Asset Readiness

## Inspect

- Decision maker, stakeholders, approval path, deadline, and selected scope.
- Display/legal name, phone, email, address or service area, hours, services/products, prices intended for publication, policies, and primary conversion.
- Domain, DNS, hosting, CMS, business profile, Search Console, analytics, social, ordering/booking, and account ownership. Record access status, never passwords or tokens.
- Logo source files, brand system, licensed photography/video, menus/service lists, approved testimonials, legal copy, and accessibility needs.
- Target customers, geography, differentiators, competitors/references, required functionality, prohibited content, content owner, revisions, and third-party costs.

Classify each item as verified, supplied-unverified, missing, not applicable, or blocked by access. Customer-affecting facts require explicit verification before launch.

## Output

Give a readiness percentage only if its weighting is shown. Prefer a phase decision: ready for discovery, ready for architecture, ready for build with placeholders, or launch-blocked. List blockers, owner, requested artifact, verification method, and next action.
