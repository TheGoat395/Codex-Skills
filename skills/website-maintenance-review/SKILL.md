# Website Maintenance Review

Start from the maintenance agreement and stack. Separate included work, recommended change requests, and items owned by the client or another provider.

## Review

- Availability, certificate status, canonical redirects, and critical production errors.
- Primary conversions and third-party booking/order/contact paths.
- Customer-facing phone, address/service area, hours, prices, services, and policies against the approved source.
- Broken links, missing media, unresolved placeholders, spam/abuse signals, and accessibility regressions.
- Analytics and Search Console anomalies where authorized; do not treat traffic fluctuation alone as a defect.
- Dependency/CMS advisories, supported runtimes, provider deprecations, and secret exposure without performing uncontrolled upgrades.
- Database/media backups for stateful systems, retention, restore ownership, and last tested restoration.
- Recent production changes, approvals, rollback, account ownership, and access continuity.

## Output

Report service health, urgent incidents, work completed, evidence, deferred or out-of-scope recommendations, responsible owner, and next review date. Do not promise unlimited support or make consequential external changes without authorization.
