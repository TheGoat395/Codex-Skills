# Release handoff template

Record: delivery classification; scope and exclusions; repository and branch; immutable release/build identifier; environments and URLs; ownership matrix; secrets owner without secret values; CMS and content owner; domain/DNS owner; analytics/consent owner; forms and notification owner; build/test/visual/accessibility/performance/SEO evidence; monitoring checks and alert destination; backup/restore evidence; rollback command and responsible person; third-party licenses; support window; unresolved risks; and every untested surface.

Never promote local, structural, or one-time test evidence into live production verification. A rollback plan is incomplete until its target artifact and owner are known.
