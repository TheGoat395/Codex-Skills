# Local Business Website Workflow

Coordinate the project; route specialist work to narrower skills instead of duplicating it.

## Source of truth

Read the project's website canon when present. Treat verified client information as authoritative. Mark unknown business facts as missing; never invent hours, prices, reviews, services, addresses, policies, or account ownership.

## Lifecycle

1. **Intake:** establish decision maker, goals, business facts, assets, account ownership, approval path, deadline, and constraints. Use `client-intake-asset-readiness`.
2. **Scope:** document pages, features, content responsibility, revisions, third-party costs, integrations, launch support, and maintenance.
3. **Plan:** define primary conversion, secondary conversions, information architecture, mobile behavior, content requirements, visual direction, and measurement plan.
4. **Build:** use the approved starter and specialist implementation skills. Keep secrets out of source control.
5. **Verify:** inspect rendered mobile and desktop output. Run accessibility, performance, SEO, conversion, security, privacy, and launch checks appropriate to the site.
6. **Launch:** protect existing DNS/email, verify HTTPS and redirects, test production conversions and analytics, and confirm indexing configuration.
7. **Handoff:** record ownership, access delegation, deployment, environment variables, known limitations, approvals, rollback, and maintenance scope.
8. **Maintain:** schedule checks based on the service agreement; do not imply unlimited support.

## Gates

- Do not begin final copy or structured data with unverified facts.
- Do not launch with unresolved placeholders, broken primary conversions, exposed secrets, or unknown domain ownership.
- Do not enable tracking before required approval/consent controls exist.
- Consequential external changes require explicit authorization and a rollback record.

## Output

Report current phase, verified inputs, missing decisions, risks, next actions, owners, and launch blockers. Separate required work from optional improvements.
