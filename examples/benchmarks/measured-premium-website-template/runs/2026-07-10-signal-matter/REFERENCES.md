# Reference Notes

The benchmark brief used general interaction and composition principles from current creative-development galleries. These links document the research boundary; the benchmark does not reproduce their code, assets, branding, or exact layouts.

- [MotionSites](https://motionsites.ai/) - motion-led website discovery
- [Landing Love](https://www.landing.love/) - animated landing-page patterns
- [Quentin Hocde 2026 on Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/quentin-hocde-2026) - interactive portfolio and gallery patterns
- [Victor Sin 2026 on Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/victor-sin-2026) - pointer, transition, and mobile-layout patterns

Borrowed principles were limited to broad ideas: a strong first-viewport identity, interactive procedural media, visible section progression, purposeful pointer response, and responsive motion restraint. Signal / Matter is a fictional original brand with original copy and implementation.
